## Getting Started

AI safety focuses on minimizing harm from model outputs and ensuring systems behave within intended bounds.

---

## Key concerns

- bias in training data
- privacy and data handling
- harmful or misleading outputs

---

## Mitigation techniques

- sanitize training and evaluation data
- use guardrails and content filters
- monitor for unsafe outputs

---

## Human oversight

Design systems so humans can review and intervene when necessary.

---

## Iteration

Continuously test against new failure modes and update models, prompts, and safety checks.
