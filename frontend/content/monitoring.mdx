## Getting Started

Monitoring helps you detect problems, understand performance, and respond to incidents. It includes metrics, logs, and alerting.

---

## Key observability signals

- **Metrics**: numeric measurements like latency and error rate
- **Logs**: event records that capture details and context
- **Traces**: requests across distributed systems

---

## Metrics

Track service-level indicators such as response time, throughput, and availability.

---

## Alerting

Alert on meaningful thresholds, not every minor change. Use alerts for urgent issues and dashboards for trends.

---

## Tools

Common tools include Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, and New Relic.

---

## Health checks

Use readiness and liveness checks to help orchestrators route traffic only to healthy instances.
