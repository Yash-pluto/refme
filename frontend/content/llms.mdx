## Getting Started

Large language models (LLMs) are foundation models trained on vast amounts of text. They can generate coherent text, answer questions, summarize content, and perform many natural language tasks.

---

## What is an LLM?

LLMs are typically transformer-based neural networks. They predict the next token in a sequence and can be conditioned on prompts or context.

Key attributes:

- large parameter counts
- pretrained on diverse corpora
- adaptable through prompt design or fine-tuning

---

## Prompting LLMs

Prompts define the task and shape the output. Good prompts are:

- clear and concise
- specific about format and constraints
- accompanied by examples when needed

Example:

```text
Translate the following sentence to French:

English: "The weather is nice today."
French:
```

---

## Output behavior

LLMs are probabilistic. They generate outputs based on token likelihood, so results may vary each call unless you fix the seed or control temperature.

Common parameters:

- `temperature`: controls randomness
- `top_p`: controls sampling diversity
- `max_tokens`: limits output length

---

## Strengths and limitations

Strengths:

- fluent text generation
- strong pattern recognition
- flexible task handling

Limitations:

- can hallucinate facts
- may repeat or drift from instructions
- sensitive to prompt phrasing

---

## Safety and guardrails

When using LLMs in products:

- filter or review sensitive outputs
- use content policies and guardrails
- avoid exposing private or regulated data in prompts
- log prompts and responses for auditing

---

## Practical applications

LLMs power:

- chatbots and assistants
- summarization and note-taking
- code completion and generation
- semantic search and retrieval

---

## Deployment patterns

Use LLMs with retrieval or external knowledge to improve grounding. Combine them with validation layers and human review for mission-critical flows.

---

## Monitoring and evaluation

Track:

- response quality
- latency
- token usage
- error or unsafe output rates

Improve systems through prompt iteration, fine-tuning, or retrieval augmentation.
