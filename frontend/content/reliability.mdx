## Getting Started

Reliability means users can depend on your system to work correctly and consistently, even when parts fail.

---

## Fault tolerance

Design systems to handle failures without affecting the overall experience.

- use retries with exponential backoff
- isolate failures to prevent cascading outages
- degrade gracefully when dependencies fail

---

## Redundancy

Keep multiple copies of critical components so a single failure does not take the system down.

---

## Recovery

Plan for fast recovery with automated restarts, backups, and rollbacks.

---

## Observability

Use monitoring, logging, and tracing to detect issues quickly and verify reliability improvements.
