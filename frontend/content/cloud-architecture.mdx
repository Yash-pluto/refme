## Getting Started

Cloud architecture designs systems that take advantage of cloud services, managed infrastructure, and elastic scaling.

---

## Cloud trade-offs

- managed services reduce operational burden
- vendor-specific tools can accelerate development
- build for failure because cloud infrastructure can still fail

---

## Resilience

Design systems to recover from failures with retries, redundancy, and isolation.

---

## Scalability

Use horizontal scaling, managed databases, and serverless services to grow without large upfront infrastructure costs.

---

## Multi-region

Use multiple regions for disaster recovery and low-latency delivery.

---

## Cost control

Monitor usage, manage reserved capacity, and choose the appropriate services for workload patterns.
