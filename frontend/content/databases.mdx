## Getting Started

Databases store and manage application data. Choosing the right database depends on your access patterns, consistency needs, and scale.

---

## Relational databases

Use SQL databases when data is structured and relationships matter.

```sql
SELECT * FROM users WHERE active = true;
```

---

## NoSQL databases

NoSQL stores are useful for flexible schemas and high write throughput.

- document stores: MongoDB, Couchbase
- key-value stores: Redis, DynamoDB
- wide-column stores: Cassandra

---

## Transactions

Use transactions to keep related operations atomic and consistent.

---

## Indexes

Index columns used frequently in queries to improve read performance.

---

## Backups

Regular backups and restore plans are essential for data durability.
