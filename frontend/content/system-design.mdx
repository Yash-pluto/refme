## Getting Started

System design is about building applications that meet performance, reliability, and scalability goals. It focuses on components, data flow, and operational trade-offs.

---

## Core concepts

- **Scalability**: ability to grow with demand
- **Reliability**: ability to stay available under failure
- **Maintainability**: ability to change safely and quickly

---

## Service boundaries

Design clear boundaries between services to reduce coupling. Microservices, APIs, and message-based communication help separate concerns.

---

## Data flow

Map how data moves through the system from request to storage and back. Use caching, queues, and event streams to optimize throughput and latency.

---

## Caching & load balancing

Cache frequently read data near the client; use load balancers to distribute traffic across services.

---

## Trade-offs

Every choice has trade-offs:

- consistency vs availability
- latency vs throughput
- simplicity vs flexibility

Document trade-offs clearly so design decisions are easier to maintain.

---

## Observability

Build systems with monitoring, logging, and tracing in mind. Observability helps you diagnose failures and understand runtime behavior.
